Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026

Thank you for using MagnetSearch (referred to as “the App”). This policy explains how we handle your information. By installing and using the App, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. What We Collect

  • The App does not require an account. We do not collect or store your name, email address, phone number, identity information, location, or device identifiers (such as IMEI / OAID / Android ID).
  • Search keywords are stored only locally on your device (for in-session display and recent history). They are never transmitted to any server operated by us — the App does not operate any server.
  • Your preferences (content filter switch, language, custom source list, deduplication options, etc.) are stored only on your device, fully under your control, and are permanently deleted upon uninstall.

2. How Search Works

  • When you perform a search, your keyword is sent only as a request parameter directly to the public index site you choose (such as LinuxTracker, etc.). The App acts solely as a technical intermediary for forwarding requests and displaying results; it does not relay, cache, or record your search content.
  • The third-party index sites you choose to access may have their own privacy policies and data handling rules. Information processed through interaction with those sites is subject to each site’s own policy; we recommend you review them.

3. Third-Party SDKs and Advertising

  • The current version contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. If such components are integrated in the future, we will update this policy and notify you in a prominent manner.

4. Permissions

  • Network access permission: used to send search requests to public index sites.
  • Apart from network permission, the App does not request any sensitive permissions such as contacts, photo gallery, location, microphone, or camera.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. Since we do not collect personal information in the first place, there is no scenario in which we provide personal information to third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy

  • The App has an age rating restriction (18+), is not designed for minors, and does not knowingly collect information from minors. If a guardian discovers that a minor is using the App, please uninstall it immediately and contact us via the email address at the end of this policy.

7. Your Rights

  • You may clear your search history, modify settings, or uninstall the App at any time to delete all local data. Since we do not hold any of your data, there is no need for you to request deletion from us.

8. Data Security

  • Your data exists only on your device and never passes through any of our servers, so there is no risk of server-side leakage or misuse. Please keep your device itself secure.

9. Policy Updates

  • We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date; material changes will be brought to your attention in an appropriate manner.
